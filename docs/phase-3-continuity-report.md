# Financial continuity checkpoint

27 October 2026. This is a partial Phase 3B implementation, not Phase 3/4 acceptance.

`src/continuity.js` analyzes explicitly supplied inclusive civil dates against recorded individual instruments. It uses exact integer money, never combines balances, groups repeated bank-code/reference identities conservatively, excludes unregistered proposals, and withholds certainty for conflicting or incomplete declarations. Results distinguish potential declared coverage, potential declared shortfall and unknown coverage. All results retain `UNCONFIRMED` banking status and require human regulatory review. No established legal gap is asserted. Missing currency is not silently defaulted.

The case-authorized continuity API returns obligation and instrument revision references and does not modify case facts or evidence. Date overrides are scenario inputs, not a change to the obligation's legally applicable holding period. Analysis is bounded to 500 instruments and fails explicitly above that limit. Superseded historical records remain visible: record supersession alone does not prove that a bank deposit ceased to exist or was released.

Migration 14 adds immutable replacement events and persisted reminder preferences. Institution users propose linked predecessor/successor instruments; assigned officers or authority supervisors can record an internal documentary relationship or reject it. Institutions can withdraw. Registration requires successor document evidence, current input revision and an acyclic relationship spanning legacy and new edges. Concurrent decisions allow one successful terminal event. Registration does not supersede the predecessor, transfer money, confirm a bank deposit or complete a release. New instrument/link edits invalidate input revisions. Existing migrations and original evidence are retained.

The existing case workspace contains continuity inputs/results, replacement proposal and decision controls, bounded ordered event history and reminder preferences. Pending proposals are queried separately from historical event pages so an old response cannot be mistaken for an unresolved proposal simply because it falls on another page.

Reminders are opt-in per user/case, with one to ten day windows. A one-minute scheduler traverses at most 100 configurations per batch and rechecks active-user case authorization before delivery. It uses Asia/Kolkata civil dates and persisted per-user/date/window deduplication. Maturity and explicit open/reopened clarification deadlines are supported; responses stop clarification reminders. Messages explicitly avoid bank/legal determinations. No external email is sent. Scheduler outages retain preferences; the next successful pass can emit an overdue reminder. Notifications are stored in the existing database; stronger tamper evidence and delivery auditing remain Phase 4C work.

Remaining internal Phase 3B scope includes unified source/evaluation/administrative lifecycle timeline, evidence-supported amount distinctions beyond declared facts, and correction/missing-evidence/assignment/escalation reminder categories. Clearance delay and release-related events depend on Phase 3C. Verified regulatory pooling/holding assumptions, independent bank confirmation and external release execution remain external dependencies, not simulated successes.
